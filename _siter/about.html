About Me

I am a Year 3 Computer Science undergraduate at NUS, minoring in Interactive Media. My work runs along two tracks. The first is evaluation and retrieval — how you build a benchmark whose rankings survive a re-run, and how you ground an answer key in something mechanically decidable rather than in another model's opinion. The second is graphics and vision, from real-time rendering and mesh processing to Unreal Engine 5. They meet in my current research on video world models, where authoring a controlled scene is what makes a failure attributable to the model.
